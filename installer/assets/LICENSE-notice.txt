KRATR — About this software
============================

KRATR is a personal tool for DJ track intake into rekordbox, shared with friends
as-is. There is no warranty of any kind; use it at your own risk. It is designed to
be safe to point at a real rekordbox library — it never writes while rekordbox is
running, and it backs up your library before every write — but you are responsible
for keeping your own backups.

Bundled component: FFmpeg
-------------------------
This installer includes ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe from the FFmpeg project
(https://ffmpeg.org), used to read, inspect and convert audio. These are distributed
under the GNU General Public License (GPL) v3. The full license text is installed
alongside the application as "ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt". FFmpeg is a separate work by its
own authors and is included here unmodified for convenience.
